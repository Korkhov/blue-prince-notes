p. 9 — OTHER FLOORPLAN COMBOS
- A FOYER drafted before GREAT HALL would mean that all of its many doors would be unlocked. (Foyer: Hallway Doors are always unlocked. Great Hall: 7 Locked Doors.)
- Drafting BUNK ROOM after NURSERY will give you 10 extra steps because it counts as two separate bedroom triggers. (Nursery: Whenever you draft a Bedroom, gain 5 steps. Bunk Room: This room counts as 2 BEDROOMS.)
- You won't have to backtrack constantly to collect your gold spread from OFFICE, if you draft a CONFERENCE ROOM. (Office: Opportunity to earn and spread gold. Conference Room: Whenever items would be spread throughout the house, they are placed in this room instead.)
- The more items you have, the greater your chances of being able to create a contraption in WORKSHOP. (Attic: 8 items. Workshop: Combine inventory to create new items.)

p. 11 — OTHER EXAMPLES of connectivity
- One of the more simple examples, HER LADYSHIP'S CHAMBER has synergy with Lady Epsen's other two rooms, her BOUDOIR and her WALK-IN CLOSET. (The next time you enter BOUDOIR, gain 10 steps. The next time you enter WALK-IN CLOSET, gain 3 gems.)
- The BOILER ROOM connects ducts from room to room to run steam-power to specific destinations in the house. LABORATORY, GARAGE, LAUNDRY ROOM, FURNACE and PUMP ROOM all have uses for steam-power.
- Speaking of PUMP ROOM, this room controls the water level throughout rooms in the estate and can interact directly with THE POOL, AQUARIUM, GREENHOUSE, KITCHEN, and several other features located outside of the main house.
