## Spread 1
All he needed was to find a new clue,
If he found a new clue, he'd know what to do,
If he knew what to do, he'd likely tell you,
For you are looking for a new clue too!

*(Picture: a detective in a hat and coat, seen from behind, in front of a board of pinned photos, letters and notes. A shield sign "ROUTE 8" with the figure 8 on it.)*

## Spread 2
He had retraced his sketches and studied the files,
Reviewed all his cases and gone through the piles
Of the evidence present and precedents evident,
And retraced his steps, as it stretched on for miles.

*(Picture: stacks of files and boxes, the detective walking with an open book. Papers on the stacks, read through the magnifying glass:)*
- **SUSPECT LIST** (two copies, one partly covered): Rose Bali, Age 50 / Richard Scott, Age 12 / Frond Mayford, Age 32 / Sandra Dillon, Age 14 / Bo Lee, Age 16 / Charles Riscal, Age 12 / Christoph Clarke, Age 25
- **CORNER CASES** (clipboard): Parlor / Patio / Nook / Office / Library / Pantry. Green check marks are drawn at the left of the list.
- **DEAD CASES** (folder): Lavatory / Closet / Attic / Furnace / Solarium

## Spread 3
He had looked high and low in all of the rooms,
He had searched every corner in each of the tombs,
In this particular case, he had checked every space.
If he found a new clue, he'd know what to do.

*(Picture: the detective among stone bricks, a rook-shaped tower, a winged statue with a scepter, two tall candlesticks. A small sign: KEEP LEFT with an arrow pointing left.)*

## Spread 4
He looked and he looked under beds and in nooks,
He questioned the maids, he questioned the cooks,
It was already over when he spoke to the chauffeur,
Who told him new clues were found only in books.

*(Picture: a maid with folded arms, a man in a hat behind her, a bookcase, a table with an apple, a banana and an orange. A sign: TO TANNER FORK with an arrow.)*

## Spread 5
So tome after tome that the library loaned,
And the books that he had that he already owned,
Were searched from their covers all the way through,
If he found a new clue, he'd know what to do.

*(Picture: the detective reads a book, a stack of four books on the table, two cuckoo clocks on the wall. Spines, from top to bottom:)*
1. **TENDER CIPHER** (red)
2. **LUNAR RENAMED** (green)
3. **UNCLE AWE** (violet)
4. **DRAFT THE SIXES** (blue)

## Spread 6
He changed all the letters in the lines that he found,
He checked all the colors of the spines that were bound,
He doubled the meaning of each seasonal greeting,
And found that the diction of each word was sound.

*(Picture: the detective writes on a blackboard. A "DEAD END" road sign in the corner.)*

## Spread 7
He stayed up all night and studied the maps,
He laid out the floorplans and measured the gaps,
And if his directory was considered a trajectory,
It was nothing short of a full mental lapse.

*(Picture: a long hall of record shelves, a sign "RECOR…" over one of them; the detective squats on a tiled floor with a book; next to him a vase and a hammer.)*

## Spread 8
"A man obsessed, and cuckoo too",
They would say as he would wander through.
The best medicine is not yet evidence,
If he found a new clue, he'd know what to do.

*(Picture: a crowd of men in hats; the detective carries books, one open. A sign above: EACH.)*

## Spread 9
And having read this book so many times,
Having scoured over each of its cryptic lines,
Have you exhausted your search?
Have you seen all the signs?
