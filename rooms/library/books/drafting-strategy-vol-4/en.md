## p. 4-5 THE GUIDING COMPASS
*An Introduction to Drafting Items*
(Side text: ITEMS THAT AFFECT SPECIAL FLOORPLAN COSTS / ITEMS THAT AFFECT DRAWING AND THE DRAFT POOL)

**GEMS** — You can't have a conversation about drafting items without starting with GEMS. These valuable stones allow architects to draft special floorplans.

**STOPWATCH** — A more short-term *(very short-term)* alternative to paying gem costs is utilizing a STOPWATCH to bypass any gem costs you encounter during a frantic one-minute drafting dash.

**EMERALD BRACELET** — Of course, if you are wealthy enough to be able to afford to purchase it from the SHOWROOM, this very high-end luxury item removes the gem cost of special floorplans altogether.

**REPELLENT SPRAY** — But why remove gem costs when you can just remove the whole room? REPELLENT SPRAY can free you of that annoying floorplan for up to a week!

**IVORY DICE** — DICE are a resource that allow you to REROLL for new floorplans while drafting.

**BRASS COMPASS** — A useful drafting tool that adjusts the orientation of floorplans. If a room has another exit, it will have a high chance to lead **north**.

**SILVER KEY** — The orientation of floorplans matters a lot less when rooms have tons of doors! SILVER KEY all but guarantees that your draft will result in a room with enough doors to ensure they lead where you want them to go.

**GEAR WRENCH** — The first item that can adjust the RARITY of floorplans in the draft pool. Although it only works on MECHANICAL ROOMS, it's a useful tool for customizing your experience.

**ORNATE COMPASS** — Another luxury item from the SHOWROOM, and likely the most useful drafting tool on this list. ORNATE COMPASS allows you to ROTATE FLOORPLANS as you draft them, allowing you to position doors to your advantage.

## p. 8-9 IT'S ABOUT TIME FOR AN UPGRADE
*Architectural Digest's Guide to Upgrade Disks*
Perhaps the most exciting item an architect can come across is an UPGRADE DISK. This floppy data disk houses a wellspring of unique architectural plans designed explicitly for a specific room. By inserting the disk into a computer terminal, architects gain the ability to shape the destiny of a room, guided by the enticing designs contained on the disk. This myriad of options not only allows architects to craft floorplans that align with their preferred strategies but also enables them to adapt their creations to evolving project requirements. Furthermore, the UPGRADE DISK's array of plans ensures that no two architects will follow the same path, fostering a landscape of diverse architectural designs.

**A FEW EXAMPLES OF ROOM UPGRADES**
- STOREROOM: +2 key, +1 gem, +1 coin / +1 key, +2 gems, +1 coin / +1 key, +1 gem, +10 coins

## p. 10-11 TOOL TIPS
*Tricks for Itemized Success*
Stringing together adjoining HALLWAYS has always been the most straightforward strategy when using a **HALL PASS**. Drafting floorplans like TUNNEL and SECRET PASSAGE are an easy way to set up longer chains of HALLWAYS.

You can adjust the settings in the terminal located in SECURITY to make Security doors more or less common. If you have found a **KEYCARD**, it is worth changing these settings to high, and if you haven't you may want to set them to low.

For the endeavoring gardener, a **WATERING CAN** is a fundamental tool. But did you know, you can refill this item with 💧? All you need to do is find a faucet with running water.

Most GREEN ROOMS can only be drafted along the edges of your house and the SECRET GARDEN is no exception. You can use this key on any locked door leading to one of these room positions.

If you have come across any fancy ruby socketed candlesticks, you may want to take note in case you can find an item that can produce a flame. These specific candlesticks contain mechanical triggers that are tripped when the candle is lit.

## p. 13 WORD FROM THE EDITOR
(Left page: drawing of a T-square, a ruler and a set square.)
Tools are cool. But they are only as good as the architect who harnesses their capabilities. As you finish reading this issue of "DRAFTING STRATEGY," I want you to open up your menu and look at your inventory. Take a moment to look at each item, and contemplate the true potential they all hold. The same potential you see there is the same potential that resides within yourself. For it is not the tools themselves that will sculpt the grand structures and monuments of tomorrow, but the deft hands and brilliant minds of architects like you.
