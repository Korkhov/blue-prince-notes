THE SIGIL — To understand a Realm Sigil, we must first dismantle the symbol and identify the individual elements that make up the whole. Each Sigil is composed of 4 things: The Core · The Dividing Lines (Rays) · The Motes · The Border.

1. THE CORE — At the center of each sigil is a unique shape, different for each realm. If the core gives us a name then the rest of the sigil tells us the history behind that name. Still, it is important to be able to identify and distinguish the core as a first step in deciphering any sigil as all the other parts are meaningless if we don't first know the place we are describing.
"Several exam[ples]… from well k[nown realms]": tall hexagon — Orin[da Aries]; pentagon — Fe[nn Aries]; hourglass — Er[aja]; heart/chevron — Cor[arica]; mountain/crown — Ar[ch Aries]. (Names cut off by the torn page.)

Weather (ray style):
- Wavy lines are used to indicate ex[treme] HEAT. The scorched air and blazi[ng sun] cause the otherwise straight rays to app[ear] shimmering.
- A RAINY realm is indicated with rays of three vertical lines *pouring* down.
- For more extreme STORMY weather, a jagged lightning shaped ray is used.
- A ray dotted with falling circles is used for colder realms in which SNOW is a common occurence.
- WINDY weather is displayed on sigils by the use of bent curved lines, rays buckling under the high winds.

3. MOTES — On each sigil, floating between the rays are small figures called motes. From the shape and style of the motes used on a sigil we can tell the type of society a given realm proclaimed, if they considered themselves mighty in arms or blessed by artistic and economic fortune.
- (✱) Tribal – A primitive realm. Hunter and gatherers.
- (seed) Agricultural – Harvest and farming based society and culture.
- (W) Martial – Militari[stic] … in generation spanning arguments.
- (boot/building) Metropolitan – Large city-state with a dense, urban and populous center.
- (III) Academic – A center for knowledge, advanced in various scholarly pursuits. These realms are home to many schools, universities and libraries.
- (square with dot) Industrial – Engineering driven culture with a focus on manufacturing, invention and production.
- (wheel) Spiritual – An elevated reverence for nature, animals and the forces that guide us.
- (flower/blot) Poetic – A strong focus on the advancement of the arts, writing, music, and language.

4. BORDER — Around each sigil is an adorned edge serving as a container for the information within. There are eight different borders, each one corresponding to a different color. These borders always correspond to the banner and national colors of that specific realm.
- black — dots of varying size
- red — wavy/flame pattern
- violet — dashed blocks
- pink — stepped/crenellated line
- green — diagonal hatching
- yellow — zig-zag triangles
- orange — small triangles
- white — small circles

PUTTING IT ALL TOGETHER — By studying the core, rays, motes and border of a sigil, you should now be able to unlock the meaning behind what had previously been an undecipherable enigma.
