Mora Jai Box
THE ORINDA ARIES VARIATION

Due to the overwhelming amount of artifacts bearing the Orinda Aries sigil that were destroyed after the war, Mora Jai puzzle boxes featuring the infamous black symbol are among the rarest (and most valuable) collectors can acquire.

When BLACK TILES are pressed, the entire row shifts to the right, as if on train rails.
