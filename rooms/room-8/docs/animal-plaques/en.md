# Room 8: animal plaques (photo order)

1. **Penguin** — THE SUM OF MY SIN / AS IT IS PENNED / IS A NUMBER WITHIN / THE ROOM WE ARE IN
2. **Lion** — TO FIRST BEGIN / PLACE ME WITHIN / A BIN WITH AN "N" / WITHIN ITS SIN
3. **Swan** — I RECOMMEND / YOU THINK AGAIN / IF I AM EVER IN / A BIN FAR FROM THE FIN
4. **Dog** — YOU WON'T FIND MY BIN / IN BETWEEN SIN / FOR I DESERVE TO BE IN / A BIN DOWN ON THE END
5. **Rabbit** — I SHARE A TREND / WITH MY NEIGHBOR'S SIN / A LETTER AND ITS TWIN / APPEAR ON EACH BIN
6. **Elephant** — TO MY CHAGRIN / MY NEIGHBOR'S SIN / IS WRITTEN THIN / TO FIT IT ALL IN
7. **Bear** — IF WE SPEND A MIN OR TEN / LOUNGING IN THE DEN / YOU'LL KNOW WHICH BIN / I'M OFTEN IN
8. **Monkey** — THE BIN I TEND / TO WIND UP IN / IS NEAR THE BEND / IN THE TAIL OF THE SKIN
