*(Seven stained-glass windows of winged figures, numbered I–VII in a medallion at the bottom. No other text.)*

- I: straw-haired figure in a red apron with a hoe; green background.
- II: figure in a chef's hat holding a long-handled paddle; blue background.
- III: figure in a red shirt and purple overalls with a long rake/pitchfork; light-blue background.
- IV: figure in a top hat with a chimney brush; yellow background.
- V: figure in a grey dress holding a broom; red background.
- VI: rider in a red hunting jacket, white breeches and black boots, holding a riding crop; lilac background.
- VII: crowned figure in white armour/robe with a sceptre and a book; red background (large window above a door).
