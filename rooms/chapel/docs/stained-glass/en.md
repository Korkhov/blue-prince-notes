Seven stained-glass windows, numbered I–VII (left → right):
I — gardener with a hoe (yellow halo)
II — cook/chef with a paddle (orange sunburst halo)
III — farmer/groundskeeper with a pitchfork, red shirt and overalls (yellow halo)
IV — chimney sweep with brushes (orange halo)
V — maid with a broom (yellow halo)
VI — huntsman/rider in a red coat with a riding crop (orange halo)
VII — crowned figure with sceptre and book, black cape (yellow halo)
