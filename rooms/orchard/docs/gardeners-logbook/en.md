GARDENER'S LOGBOOK — April 10th, 1992 — Robert Nogula, Groundskeeper.
- When I first came on the estate… my predecessor had kept no notes… I will make an effort to record my dealings on the estate as it pertains to horticulture.
- Among my many commissioned duties… maintenance of the west grounds has not been a priority since Lady Epsen's death and the closure of the West Wing. I brush the west path weekly. There are eight green rooms within the house… only draftable on the perimeter of the house along the East and West Wings: Terrace, Patio, Veranda, Morning Room, Greenhouse, Secret Garden… all fall within this category.
- THE SOIL SURVEY: surveyed the soil of the plots in the East and West wings; soil quality affects fruit yield and the amount of flowers in the Greenhouse. (Survey table: see ru.md)
- SMUDGE POTS: A few years ago the Baron switched the house to electric power… but everything outside is still powered by gasoline, including the orchard's smudge pots. When not in use, the orchard's gasline valve should be turned off.
- Cloister: would like to replace the statue of Orinda in the Cloister with the angel of gardening, Ryuna (seen in the chapel). "And she wonders why I avoid tending the Cloister? (Truth be told, I have forgotten how to access the inner courtyard…)"
