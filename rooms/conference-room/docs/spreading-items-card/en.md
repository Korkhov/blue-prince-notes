**SPREADING ITEMS**

*(Icon: arrows pointing outwards in all directions.)*

SOME SPECIAL ROOMS ALLOW YOU TO "SPREAD" ITEMS THROUGHOUT YOUR HOUSE.

WHEN ITEMS ARE "SPREAD" THEY ARE IMMEDIATELY PLACED IN SEVERAL OF THE ROOMS CURRENTLY ON YOUR ESTATE.

HOWEVER, IF YOU HAVE A CONFERENCE ROOM, SPREAD ITEMS ARE GATHERED IN THAT ROOM INSTEAD.
