# Letter from Mary to Herbert

(beginning cut off: "Dea…")

Herbert,

Thank you so much for your gift of the "wedding doves," whom I, of course, recognized immediately. And, yes, I got the hint. So here's your letter. I know it's long, long overdue.

The [married?] life suits me I think. Not that I didn't love Mount Holly, of course, but after years of being confined in a tiny dormitory, the small place Daniel and I have picked out in Reddington feels more like a stately manor! Once we've finished unpacking and setting everything up, I'll insist on you making an exception to your reclusive lifestyle to come, share a meal and admire our somewhat less impressive but much more personal view of the town.

Thanks again for the card,
Mary

*(the word before "life" is hard to read; "mahew" / "married")*
