*(Handwritten on a white sheet; under it a drawn shape, an elongated figure with notches.)*

Southward I see a swan,
Ashen like sands of the shore,
Carried by westwardly winds,
Rogue like the moon of the north.
Eastward I see a crow,
Dark are the days coming fourth.

*(Photo 2: on the table next to the poem, a grey stone shard with a flower carved on it — a central circle with round petals — plus a small circle, and a silver swan figurine.)*
