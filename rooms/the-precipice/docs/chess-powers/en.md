# Powers of the chess pieces (The Precipice)

1. **AMBITION OF THE PAWN** — When you reach Rank 8 each day, you may choose Knight, Bishop, Rook or Queen. Gain the power of that piece until the end of the day.
2. **MANTLE OF THE KNIGHT** — Add THE ARMORY to your draft pool.
3. **GAMBIT OF THE QUEEN** — Each time you draft a room on the Queenside (West Wing) of your house, lose 5 steps and gain 2 keys.
4. **BANNER OF THE KING** — Each day, you may choose a color. Rooms of the chosen color are more likely to be drawn while drafting.
5. **RESILIENCE OF THE ROOK** — You may draw floorplans up to 4 times when drafting the 4 corner rooms of your house.
6. **PIETY OF THE BISHOP** — You do not have to pay tithes. Instead, gain 30 coins the first time you enter CHAPEL each day with this power.

Each screen asks: "Do you accept this power?" YES / NO.
