# Her Ladyship's Sleep DIARY (Lady Clara Epsen, January 1979)
1-17-79 — After countless sleepless nights and failed home remedies, my physician has urged me to document my sleeping routines in the various bedrooms of our estate…
1-18-79 My Room (Her Ladyship's Chamber) — Sleep 2.5 h, peaceful despite brevity. Effects: woke with 10 less steps but observed an additional star in the sky.
1-21-79 Guest Bedroom — Sleep 4 h, very fragmented. Effects: nothing special.
1-22-79 Bunk Room — "It was strange to sleep in the childhood room of my late husband… growing up with an elder brother… my thoughts of Simon kept me up." Sleep twice the usual, two times as restless. Effects: visited the family tomb, found a flower already laid on his grave.
1-24-79 Servant's Quarters (Hartley's room, "roll the die") — Sleep ~1 h, terrible. Effects: about half the steps next day; found a stray die in my pocket.
1-25-79 Master Bedroom — Sleep 8 h, best in months. Effects: more energy and more steps than normal.
1-28-79 — My beloved daughter has given birth to a son, and has requested my presence in town… For now, I shall take my leave of the bedrooms of Mount Holly. — Lady Clara Epsen, Grandmother
Last page: unlabeled sketch (square building, path, bridge over a ravine, road).
