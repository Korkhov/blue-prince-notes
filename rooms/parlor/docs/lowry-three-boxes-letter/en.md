May the 2nd

Dear Herbert:

It was a great honor receiving the invitation to dine at your estate with the Oldhams this past Thursday. If I'm being frank, it had been quite a while since we had been seen in decent society and we were beginning to worry that my wife's wit had lost its charm (I have no delusions that my company is rival to hers!)

If I can refresh your memory, we both spent much of that evening discussing our mutual love of games and puzzles and I had mentioned to you a rather singular parlor game I had played some years back involving three boxes of different colors. You had pressed me to write with the particulars of this game (when I found the time) and I have included with this letter all the details you will need to share with guests should you be inclined to play this confounding game.

Always your friend,
*M. Lowry*
