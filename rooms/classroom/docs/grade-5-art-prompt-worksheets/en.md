# Grade 5: Art Prompt worksheets

ART PROMPT
Directions: Draw two things that share all but one letter of their name.

**1. Name: Gregory.** Drawing: a robot standing on a line, and below the line a tree root. Teacher (red): "Clever!" and a large "B".

**2. Name: Laura A.** Drawing: an acorn and an ear of corn. Teacher (red): "Fantastic job!" and "A".

**3. Name: JOSH.** Drawing: two scribbled shapes on the left, a tooth in the middle, a bent shape on the right. Teacher (red): "spelling incorrect" and "F".
