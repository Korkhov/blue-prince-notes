Four display cases with crests (and busts):
1. **MARIGOLD**: crest only, an empty case with a small disc at the bottom (no bust). Left half: diagonal stripes with holly and berries; right half: swan over water, two spades.
2. **JONES**: bust of a boy in a school uniform and tie. Crest: swan over water and a spade, holly stripes, a treble clef.
3. **SINCLAIR**: bust of a man in a collared shirt. Crest: diagonal stripes with holly and berries.
4. **EPSEN**: bust of a woman with a bow tie. Crest: swan over water and two spades.
