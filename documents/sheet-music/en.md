2: "Among this scattered symphony… A message hidden beneath the keys… In bold words sung by you and me…"
3: "The ones of you who oft assume… That you alone can solve this tune…"

5: "Trees found inside will never shade… These sheets you find a worthy trade"
6: "Under these stars, the dance is held…"
7: "Two remain for one last dance… The true time lost in past romance."
8: "Stones cut by hand, and letters wrought… A message read is often lost… Among the bold words, spoken soft…"
