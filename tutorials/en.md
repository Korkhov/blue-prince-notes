In-game tutorial cards:
01 A JOURNEY NOTEWORTHY — consider keeping a journal for notes and observations.
02 DRAWING FLOORPLANS — each time you click on a door you draw three floorplans and must select one; some items/rooms let you REDRAW.
03 SPREADING ITEMS — special rooms spread items throughout your house; with a Conference Room, spread items gather there instead.
04 ADDITIONAL READING — the House Directory (press RB) has info about each room.
05 SLEDGE HAMMER — can shatter padlocks on locked trunks.
